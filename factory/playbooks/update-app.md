# Playbook: change an app (interactive)

1. Read `apps/<slug>/SPEC.md`, `app.json` and `CHANGELOG.md`.
2. Say in two or three plain sentences what will change, and update `SPEC.md` if the change
   adds or alters a requirement. Wait for an OK.
3. Create a branch `app/<slug>-<short-name>`. Act as the **builder**.
4. Act as the **reviewer**. Fix anything marked "Needs work".
5. Show the result at phone and laptop widths, then ask before committing or opening a PR.
