# Changelog

## 0.1.0

- First version: a neon theatre marquee whose signs spin like pokie reels. Pull the lever
  (drag, tap or space), pick a category at the box office, spin as often as you like.
  86 movies across 7 categories.
- Theatre front closely follows the Atlas 9 reference; "Now showing" sits above the title,
  the left board shows the category, and the side boards start blank.
- Simplified to a pure spinner: no watched list, no buttons after landing, no counts.
  Titles always fit the sign; the marquee is centred; two typefaces throughout.
